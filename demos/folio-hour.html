Questions people ask first

Do members need to pay?

No. Only the person hosting the club needs a paid plan. Everyone they invite reads for free.

Does it work with print books?

Yes. Members can log their page and add notes by page number, so print and ebook readers can share one discussion.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings, and your clubs stay readable until the end of your billing period.
